From can to clear record.

A quick walkthrough of dairy intake, from farmer selection to the daily summary.

All names, readings, and the ₹40/L example rate below are fictional. Screenshots use a test workspace. Your working app starts empty.

1

Choose the farmer

Search by numeric ID, or register a farmer offline. A repeat visit needs confirmation. The active session and rate stay visible.

2

Measure and decide

Enter this can’s volume and fat. Select the whole-can decision and save using the bottom action. Each saved can stays on the phone; unfinished measurements remain a draft.

3

Explain a rejection

Choose a rejection reason. Attach and preview a compressed photo, or give an explicit explanation when a photo is unavailable. Unmeasured fat is distinct from zero.

4

Review the daily ledger

Accepted liters determine the estimated payout at each delivery’s preserved rate. Corrections retain originals; voids preserve the entry and remove it from totals.

Before going offline, set up this phone online and wait for “Offline app ready”. Records and photos synchronize separately while the app is open. Clearing browser data loses pending records and workspace access.
